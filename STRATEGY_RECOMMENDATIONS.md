## 🔍 Key Analytical Findings & Evidence

### 1. Profitability Compression Drivers (Why Margin is Dropping)
- **Discount Waterfall Leakage**: Gross list prices are diluted by an aggregate **14.2% discount rate** across contract discounts, promotional markdown, and ad-hoc digital coupons.
- **Cost-to-Serve Disparity**: E-commerce fulfillment in Tier 3 cities incurs a **+65% cost overrun** relative to Tier 1 cities, eroding net pocket margin on orders under ₹999 to < 4.2%.

### 2. Price Elasticity & Pricing Power
- Econometric Log-Log OLS regressions reveal that **Home & Kitchen** (\(\epsilon = -0.82\)) and **FMCG** (\(\epsilon = -0.58\)) categories are **price inelastic**.
- OmniRetail list prices in Home & Kitchen are currently indexed at **0.91 vs competitors** (Reliance Retail & Amazon India), leaving uncaptured pricing power.

### 3. Promotional Efficiency
- **End-of-Season Sale (EOSS)** yields a **negative incremental ROI (-0.32x)** because ~60% of promotional volume subsidizes baseline demand without generating net incremental margin.
- In contrast, **Diwali Festive Dhamaka** yields **+2.45x ROI** due to a 30% vendor co-op rebate offset.

### 4. Customer Whale Curve Dilution
- The top **20% of customers generate 82% of net pocket profit**.
- **12% of B2B Enterprise accounts are Value Destroyers** (negative pocket margin) due to legacy contract discounts (>22%) combined with volume rebates and high logistics costs.

## ⚠️ Risks, Assumptions & Mitigation Framework

1. **Risk**: Customer churn among B2B Enterprise accounts facing contract discount caps.
   - *Mitigation*: Introduce minimum annual order volume thresholds (e.g., ₹50 Lakhs) to qualify for tier-1 contract discounts.
2. **Risk**: E-commerce traffic deceleration upon EOSS markdown removal.
   - *Mitigation*: Replace flat clearance discounts with vendor-co-funded bundle offers.
3. **Limitation**: Synthetic transaction model assumes stable macro macroeconomic trends in India.
