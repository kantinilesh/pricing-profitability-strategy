## 🔬 Methodological Rigor & Causality Disclaimer

- **Matched Control Baseline**: Baseline sales velocity is computed per category using non-promoted control transactions (`Baseline / None`).
- **Correlation vs Causation Note**: Calculated promotional lift reflects observational transaction decomposition relative to matched baseline averages rather than a randomized clinical A/B test. Unobserved external factors (e.g. festive holiday timing) may influence observed sales velocity.

## 💡 Actionable Strategic Recommendations

### 1. STOP: End of Season Sale (EOSS) Clearance Markdowns
- **Decision**: **STOP** un-funded quarterly EOSS clearance sales.
- **Evidence**: EOSS creates negative gross profit (-₹5.87 Crore) and operates at an ROI of **-1.88x**, subsidizing baseline demand.
- **Action**: Replace flat markdowns with targeted vendor-co-funded bundle clearance for obsolete SKUs only.

### 2. MODIFY: Flash Wednesday Digital Promotions
- **Decision**: **MODIFY** Flash Wednesday into a Direct App/Web Exclusive.
- **Evidence**: Flash Wednesday delivers a healthy **29.66% gross margin on Direct App/Web**, but drops to **2.78% margin on Marketplace** due to 10% commission fees.
- **Action**: Restructure Flash Wednesday as an exclusive loyalty reward on OmniRetail's direct app.

### 3. CONTINUE: Festive Dhamaka (Diwali / Navratri)
- **Decision**: **CONTINUE & SCALE** Festive Dhamaka campaigns.
- **Evidence**: 35% manufacturer co-op funding offsets discount spend, achieving an incremental ROI of **+1.45x** in high-margin categories.
- **Action**: Reallocate trade spend saved from stopping EOSS to double down on Festive Dhamaka co-op promotions.
