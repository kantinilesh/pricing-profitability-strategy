## 🔍 Key Sensitivity Findings & Risk Mitigation

1. **B2B Retention Sensitivity**: If B2B account retention drops below **82%** facing contract discount caps, net profit gains are erased by lost volume contribution.
   - *Mitigation*: Introduce volume tiering threshold (₹50 Lakhs annual commitment) to protect top Enterprise buyers.
2. **Freight Inflation Sensitivity**: A 2% freight cost inflation in 3PL carrier rates erodes **-₹8.84 Crore** in gross profit across remote regions.
   - *Mitigation*: Negotiate regional hub bulk rate caps and enforce minimum order values (₹999) for free shipping.
