# Analytical Plan

This document maps each registered hypothesis to required datasets, analytical methods, statistical tools, visual outputs, and expected business insights.

---

| Hypothesis ID | Target Dataset | SQL / Python Analysis Method | Statistical Method | Visualization Output | Expected Business Insight |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **H1 (Off-Invoice Leakage)** | `fact_transactions`, `dim_products` | Aggregate discounts by channel and product category; compute discount waterfall metrics. | Descriptive statistics & Correlation analysis | Stacked Bar Chart (Discount Waterfall) & Scatterplot | Quantify ad-hoc coupon leakage in e-commerce; identify SKUs with >20% unapproved markdowns. |
| **H2 (Customer Tier Misalignment)** | `fact_transactions`, `dim_customers` | Group customer transactions by account; calculate net pocket profit per customer tier. | Pareto Distribution & Cumulative Sum ranking | Bain Customer Whale Curve Chart | Identify top 20% profit drivers vs bottom 10-15% value-destroying B2B accounts. |
| **H3 (Promo Demand Subsidization)** | `fact_transactions`, `dim_promotions` | Calculate baseline non-promo daily sales; measure incremental volume lift and trade spend ROI. | Pre/Post Event Difference-in-Differences | Grouped Bar Chart (Incremental ROI vs Trade Spend) | Pinpoint negative-ROI clearance sales (e.g. EOSS) subsidizing baseline demand. |
| **H4 (Tier 3 Logistics Overrun)** | `fact_transactions`, `dim_customers`, `dim_stores` | Calculate fulfillment cost as % of net revenue across city tiers and order value brackets. | ANOVA & Kruskal-Wallis non-parametric test | Heatmap (Channel x City Tier Pocket Margin) | Reveal last-mile shipping cost overruns in Tier 3 e-commerce for orders < ₹999. |
| **H5 (Inelastic Price Power)** | `fact_transactions`, `dim_products`, `fact_competitor_prices` | Estimate log-log regression of sales volume on effective unit price with promo controls. | Log-Log OLS Linear Regression (\(\ln Q = \alpha + \beta \ln P + \epsilon\)) | Scatterplot with OLS Regression Line & Price Index Bar Chart | Uncover inelastic SKUs (\(\epsilon > -0.9\)) in Home & Kitchen priced below competitors. |
| **H6 (E-Comm Apparel Returns)** | `fact_transactions`, `dim_products` | Measure return rates and reverse logistics costs by category and store channel. | Logistic Regression & Binomial Proportion Test | Bar Chart (Return Rate % & Margin Impact by Category) | Demonstrate that 18% apparel return rate eliminates net profit on e-commerce transactions. |
| **H7 (Product Mix Dilution)** | `fact_transactions`, `dim_products` | Calculate category revenue shares and gross margin contributions over time. | Shift-Share Decomposition | Area Chart (Category Share of Revenue vs Margin) | Prove top-line growth driven by low-margin Electronics dilutes company gross margin %. |
| **H8 (Marketplace Commission Drag)** | `fact_transactions`, `dim_stores` | Compare net revenue, fulfillment fees, marketplace commissions, and pocket profit across channels. | Two-sample t-test (Direct vs Marketplace Margin) | Grouped Column Chart (Unit Economics by Channel) | Show Direct App/Web yields 450 bps higher pocket margin than external marketplaces. |
| **H9 (Unclaimed Vendor Co-Op)** | `fact_transactions`, `dim_promotions` | Compare contractual vendor co-op rebate rates against actual captured vendor rebates. | Paired t-test (Contractual vs Realized Rebate) | Waterfall Chart (Gross Promo Spend to Net Co-op Recovered) | Identify 25% gap in unclaimed vendor co-op funding due to manual claim tracking. |
| **H10 (Low AOV Transaction Loss)** | `fact_transactions` | Segment transactions by AOV brackets (e.g. <₹499, ₹500-999, >₹1000); compute net pocket profit. | Segmented Cluster Analysis | Line Chart (AOV Bracket vs Pocket Margin %) | Establish that transactions under ₹499 operate at a net loss of -6.5% pocket margin. |
| **H11 (Store Sales Density)** | `fact_transactions`, `dim_stores` | Aggregate store sales per square foot and operating margin by region and city tier. | Multivariate Linear Regression | Scatterplot (Sales Density vs Store Operating Margin %) | Identify underperforming Tier 3 stores failing to cover fixed occupancy costs. |
| **H12 (Price Ending Psychology)** | `fact_transactions`, `dim_products` | Categorize SKUs by price ending (.99, .00, ad-hoc non-standard); measure conversion velocity. | Chi-Square Test of Independence | Bar Chart (Average Conversion Rate by Price Ending) | Confirm that standard .99 price endings achieve 8% higher sales velocity than non-standard prices. |
