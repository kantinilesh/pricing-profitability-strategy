## 📋 Recommendation 1: Terminate Un-Funded EOSS Clearance Markdowns

- **Action**: Immediately terminate quarterly End-of-Season Sale (EOSS) clearance markdowns (32.4% avg discount). Replace flat clearance sales with vendor-co-funded bundle offers.
- **Expected Financial Impact**: **+₹13.04 Crore net profit recovery (+245 bps gross margin expansion)**.
- **Evidence**: EOSS Clearance sales operate at a **-1.88x negative ROI**, generating negative gross profit (-₹5.87 Crore) and extreme marketplace margin erosion (-32.95%).
- **Implementation Difficulty**: **Medium**. Requires alignment with retail merchandising and promotional marketing calendars.
- **Risk**: Potential short-term volume reduction (~15% drop in clearance shoppers).
- **Time Horizon**: **30 Days (Immediate Horizon 1)**.
- **KPI to Monitor**: Net Incremental Promo ROI & Clearance Category Gross Profit.

---

## 📋 Recommendation 2: Selective +5% List Price Increase on Inelastic SKUs

- **Action**: Implement a **+5% list price increase** on top 30 SKUs in Home & Kitchen and FMCG categories.
- **Expected Financial Impact**: **+₹5.26 Crore net profit expansion (+99 bps gross margin expansion)**.
- **Evidence**: Econometric Log-Log OLS regressions confirm demand is price inelastic (\(\beta = -0.062\) for Home & Kitchen; \(\beta = -0.084\) for FMCG). Competitor price indexing reveals OmniRetail is priced 9% below market benchmark.
- **Implementation Difficulty**: **Low**. Requires updating MSRP price master tables in POS and e-commerce systems.
- **Risk**: Low risk (<0.4% estimated volume loss).
- **Time Horizon**: **30–60 Days (Horizon 2)**.
- **KPI to Monitor**: Realized Unit Price (ASP) & Category Unit Sales Velocity.

---

## 📋 Recommendation 3: Enforce 18% Contract Discount Ceiling on B2B Accounts

- **Action**: Establish an **18.0% maximum contract discount ceiling** for B2B Enterprise accounts and require minimum annual purchase commitments of ₹50 Lakhs.
- **Expected Financial Impact**: **+₹0.82 Crore net profit recovery (+15 bps gross margin expansion)**.
- **Evidence**: 84 B2B Enterprise accounts currently operate as **Value Destroyers** due to un-capped contract discounts averaging 22.75%, erasing -₹7.08 Crore in potential profit.
- **Implementation Difficulty**: **Medium-High**. Requires B2B sales team contract renegotiations and commercial policy enforcement.
- **Risk**: Customer churn among over-discounted B2B accounts (modeled at 90% retention rate).
- **Time Horizon**: **60–90 Days (Horizon 3)**.
- **KPI to Monitor**: Customer Account Gross Margin % & B2B Account Retention Rate.

---

## 📋 Recommendation 4: Restructure Digital Flash Sales to Direct App/Web Channel

- **Action**: Restructure Flash Wednesday digital promotions into exclusive loyalty rewards on Direct App/Web, eliminating marketplace promotional participation.
- **Expected Financial Impact**: **+₹2.21 Crore net profit improvement**.
- **Evidence**: Flash Wednesday achieves **29.66% gross margin on Direct App/Web**, but drops to **2.78% margin on Marketplace** due to 10% marketplace take rates and 12% fulfillment fees.
- **Implementation Difficulty**: **Low**. Digital ad spend re-allocation to Direct App campaigns.
- **Risk**: Low risk; shifts digital traffic toward higher-margin direct channels.
- **Time Horizon**: **30 Days (Horizon 1)**.
- **KPI to Monitor**: Direct App Share of Net Revenue & Channel Contribution Margin.
