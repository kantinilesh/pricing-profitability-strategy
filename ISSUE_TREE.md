## Detailed MECE Decomposition

### 1. Revenue Drivers
- **1.1 Realized Price (Net Unit Price)**
  - **1.1.1 List Price Positioning**: Is base list pricing competitive and aligned with customer willingness to pay?
  - **1.1.2 Contract Discounts**: Are contract discounts for B2B/Enterprise customer accounts overly generous relative to purchase commitments?
  - **1.1.3 Promotional Discounts**: Are campaign markdowns diluting unit revenue without driving adequate incremental volume?
  - **1.1.4 Ad-hoc Markdowns**: Are unplanned e-commerce digital coupons and discretionary store discounts leaking margin?
- **1.2 Sales Volume & Structural Mix**
  - **1.2.1 Product Mix Shift**: Is volume shifting from high-margin categories (Apparel, Home) to low-margin categories (Consumer Electronics)?
  - **1.2.2 Customer Mix Shift**: Is total revenue growing primarily through low-margin high-volume B2B accounts at the expense of high-margin retail customers?
  - **1.2.3 Geographic Mix**: Is growth concentrating in Tier 2/3 cities with different basket sizes and price sensitivities?
  - **1.2.4 Channel Mix**: Is sales volume migrating from high-margin physical stores to lower-margin e-commerce channels?

### 2. Cost Drivers
- **2.1 Variable Costs**
  - **2.1.1 Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)**: Are supplier cost increases eroding base gross margins?
  - **2.1.2 Vendor Co-op Rebates**: Are co-op funding rebates from manufacturers being fully captured to offset promotional spend?
  - **2.1.3 Trade Promotional Spend**: What is the net direct financial cost of promotional campaigns?
- **2.2 Operational Costs & Cost-to-Serve**
  - **2.2.1 Channel Fulfillment**: Are e-commerce marketplace commissions and direct warehousing costs outstripping channel revenue growth?
  - **2.2.2 Freight & Logistics**: Are last-mile logistics costs for long-distance Tier 3 city delivery diluting transaction margin?
  - **2.2.3 Returns & Restocking**: Are high product return rates in online apparel channels generating uncompensated reverse logistics expenses?
  - **2.2.4 Store Fixed Overhead**: Is physical store footfall insufficient to cover fixed retail occupancy costs?
