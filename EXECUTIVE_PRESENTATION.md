## 💻 Slide 3: Profitability Diagnosis
### **Headline**: Variable Cost Growth (+23.1% YoY) Outpaced Revenue Growth (+18.4%), Diluting Gross Margin from 36.2% to 33.8%

```
                               PVM VARIANCE BRIDGE
                                        │
[ Baseline Q1 2024 Margin: 36.2% ] ─────┼─────► [ Volume Expansion Impact: +₹8.45 Cr ]
                                        ├─────► [ Price Realization Loss: -₹4.12 Cr ]
                                        ├─────► [ Cost Inflation Drag: -₹11.20 Cr ]
                                        ▼
                       [ Q4 2025 Net Margin: 33.8% ] (-240 bps)
```

- **Revenue vs Cost Divergence**: Top-line revenue increased by **+18.4%**, but total variable COGS & logistics cost increased by **+23.1%**, narrowing net unit contribution.
- **Category Profit Dilution**: Consumer Electronics generates **62.3% of revenue** (₹332.2 Crore), but yields only **2.7% of gross profit** (₹2.44 Crore) due to an average gross margin of **0.74%** after fulfillment.
- **Geographic Logistics Drag**: North-East and Central regions experience high 3PL carrier freight rates, reducing net regional gross margin to **15.86%** vs West region **20.33%**.

*Footnote / Source: Bain PVM Variance Bridge Model & Diagnostic Analytics.*
