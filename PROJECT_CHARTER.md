# Project Charter: Pricing & Profitability Intelligence Platform

## 1. Business Problem
An omnichannel Indian retail enterprise ("OmniRetail India") operating 120+ physical retail stores across Tier 1, Tier 2, and Tier 3 cities alongside an e-commerce platform has achieved robust top-line revenue growth (+18.4% YoY). However, operating profitability has become volatile and inconsistent across product categories, customer segments, geographic regions, and distribution channels, resulting in a **240 bps net margin compression** over the past 8 quarters. 

Management lacks clear visibility into the root causes of margin leakage, the net profitability of specific customer and product combinations, the elasticity of current pricing structures, and the net incremental return on promotional spend.

---

## 2. Business Objective
The primary objective of this project is to develop an empirical, data-driven analytical platform that diagnoses the structural drivers of margin performance, evaluates pricing and promotional effectiveness, quantifies customer and product value creation, and defines a prioritized strategic roadmap for profitable growth.

Specifically, the platform aims to identify actionable opportunities to recover 250–350 bps of operating margin within 90–180 days of implementation.

---

## 3. Core Management Questions
1. **Why is profitability changing?** (Decompose gross margin and net margin drivers across revenue vs cost components, price vs volume, mix shifts, and cost-to-serve).
2. **Which drivers are responsible?** (Isolate price realization, product mix, channel economics, customer tiering, and logistics cost inflation).
3. **Are current prices and discounts appropriate?** (Evaluate category and SKU price elasticities, discount waterfall leakage, and uncaptured pricing power).
4. **Which customer/product/region combinations create the most value?** (Identify top margin accretive segments vs value-destroying accounts using Pareto 80/20 and cost-to-serve analysis).
5. **What actions should management take?** (Define quantified pricing interventions, promotional reallocation, discount governance rules, and implementation priorities).

---

## 4. Scope
- **Data Scope**: 24 months of transactional, product, customer, channel, promotional, and competitor pricing data across OmniRetail India's retail network.
- **Channels**: Physical retail stores (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3 cities), Direct E-Commerce (App & Web), and E-Commerce Marketplace channels.
- **Analytical Scope**:
  - Revenue and cost waterfall decomposition.
  - Price elasticity estimation using econometric log-log regression.
  - Customer profitability ranking and Whale Curve construction.
  - Promotional lift vs baseline subsidization modeling.
  - What-if strategic scenario simulation and margin expansion quantification.

---

## 5. Out of Scope
- Real-time dynamic automated price execution engine integration into live Point-of-Sale (POS) systems.
- Supplier renegotiation contract terms or supply chain procurement sourcing changes beyond observable COGS.
- Long-term store real estate leasing and footprint restructuring.
- Custom machine learning deep neural network customer churn forecasting.

---

## 6. Expected Management Decisions
1. **List Price Adjustments**: Selective list price increases (+3% to +7%) on identified price-inelastic SKUs and categories.
2. **Promotional Budget Reallocation**: Termination or restructuring of negative-ROI clearance campaigns (e.g., End of Season Sale markdowns) and budget shift toward high-ROI co-op funded festive promotions.
3. **Discount Governance Framework**: Establishment of strict contract discount ceilings (e.g., 18% max) and volume threshold requirements for B2B Enterprise accounts.
4. **Channel & Regional Policy**: Setting minimum order values (AOV) for Tier 3 e-commerce orders to offset high last-mile fulfillment costs.
