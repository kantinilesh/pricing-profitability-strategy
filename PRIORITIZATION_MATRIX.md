## 📌 Executive Prioritization Framework

Strategic initiatives are evaluated and prioritized using a 3-factor composite score:

$$\text{Priority Score} = \text{Impact} \times \text{Feasibility} \times \text{Confidence}$$

- **Impact**: Quantified financial net profit recovery (Scale: 1 = Low <₹1M, 5 = High >₹10M).
- **Feasibility**: Implementation ease, operational complexity, and speed of execution (Scale: 1 = Difficult, 5 = Easy).
- **Confidence**: Empirical data strength and model robustness (Scale: 1 = Low, 5 = High).
