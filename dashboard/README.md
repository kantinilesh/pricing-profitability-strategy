# Executive Business Intelligence Dashboard
## OmniRetail India | Web Application & BI Interface

---

## 📌 Dashboard Architecture

The executive BI dashboard is an interactive, multi-tab Web Application designed to support executive decision-making across 7 strategic pages:

1. **Page 1: Executive Summary** (*"Operating Margin Compressed by 240 bps Despite 18.4% Revenue Growth"*)
2. **Page 2: Profitability Diagnosis** (*"Profit Erosion is Driven by Price-Volume-Mix Leakage and Regional Logistics Overruns"*)
3. **Page 3: Pricing Strategy** (*"Uncaptured Pricing Power Exists in Price-Inelastic Home & Kitchen SKUs"*)
4. **Page 4: Products Portfolio** (*"Top 20% SKUs Generate 75.7% of Profit While 35 Dog SKUs Dilute Margin"*)
5. **Page 5: Customers Economics** (*"84 B2B Accounts Destroy ₹7.08 Crore Profit Due to Un-Capped Contract Discounts"*)
6. **Page 6: Promotions Effectiveness** (*"End of Season Clearance Markdowns Operate at -1.88x Negative ROI"*)
7. **Page 7: Strategic Scenarios** (*"Full Transformation Scenario G Recovers +359 bps (+₹19.12 Crore) Net Profit"*)

---

## 🚀 Execution & Access Instructions

To launch the local web server:

```bash
python3 -m uvicorn app.main:app --reload --port 8000
```

Access in your browser: `http://localhost:8000/`
