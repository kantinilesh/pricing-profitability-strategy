## 🔍 Detailed Archetype Analysis & Strategic Implications

### 1. Revenue & Profit Stars (Core Champions)
- **SKUs**: Top 4 SKUs (*Home Item 15*, *Footwear Item 5*, *Home Item 14*, *Apparel Item 1*).
- **Economics**: Generate **32.27% of company profit** (₹29.16 Crore) from 9.03% of revenue. Average Gross Margin is **60.82%**.
- **Strategic Recommendation**: **PROTECT AVAILABILITY & EXPAND**. Guarantee 100% in-stock rate, allocate premium shelf space in retail stores, and features on direct web homepage.

### 2. Profit Stars (Niche High-Margin SKUs)
- **SKUs**: 34 SKUs across Beauty & Cosmetics, Footwear, and Apparel.
- **Economics**: Generate **49.63% of total profit** (₹44.85 Crore) with an impressive average gross margin of **49.06%**.
- **Strategic Recommendation**: **SELECTIVE LIST PRICE INCREASE (+5%)**. High pricing power and low elasticity (\(\beta = -0.099\)). Raising prices by 5% yields **+₹2.24 Crore profit expansion**.

### 3. Volume-Heavy Low-Margin (Electronics / Bulk)
- **SKUs**: High-ticket Consumer Electronics items (*Consumer Item 15*, *Consumer Item 1*).
- **Economics**: Generate high revenue (₹41.11 Crore), but gross margin is compressed to **17.15%**.
- **Strategic Recommendation**: **FLOOR PRICING & ZERO DISCOUNTS**. Enforce strict minimum selling price floors to cover fulfillment cost-to-serve.

### 4. Low-Volume Low-Margin (Dog SKUs)
- **SKUs**: 35 low-tier SKUs in Consumer Electronics and FMCG.
- **Economics**: Consume **67.04% of top-line inventory revenue** (₹356.99 Crore), but yield only **7.67% of total company profit** (gross margin **10.99%**).
- **Strategic Recommendation**: **PORTFOLIO RATIONALIZATION / DELISTING**. Delist low-performing SKUs, negotiate supplier price concessions, or increase base list prices (+10%).
