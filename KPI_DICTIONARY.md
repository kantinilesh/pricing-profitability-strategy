# KPI Dictionary

This document provides exact mathematical formulas, business meanings, and required data fields for all core financial and analytical metrics used across the platform.

---

### 1. Gross Revenue
- **Formula**: \(\text{Gross Revenue} = \sum (\text{Quantity} \times \text{List Price})\)
- **Business Meaning**: Total top-line monetary value of merchandise sold at full sticker list price prior to any discounts or deductions.
- **Data Fields Required**: `fact_transactions.quantity`, `fact_transactions.list_price_inr`

### 2. Units Sold
- **Formula**: \(\text{Units} = \sum \text{Quantity}\)
- **Business Meaning**: Total physical quantity of products items purchased across transactions.
- **Data Fields Required**: `fact_transactions.quantity`

### 3. Average Selling Price (ASP)
- **Formula**: \(\text{ASP} = \frac{\text{Net Revenue}}{\text{Units Sold}}\)
- **Business Meaning**: Effective average net revenue realized per individual unit sold after all discount deductions.
- **Data Fields Required**: `fact_transactions.net_revenue_inr`, `fact_transactions.quantity`

### 4. List Price
- **Formula**: \(\text{List Price} = \text{Sticker Price per Unit}\)
- **Business Meaning**: Standard MSRP baseline price established by merchandising prior to contractual or promotional discounts.
- **Data Fields Required**: `dim_products.list_price_inr`

### 5. Realized Price (Pocket Price)
- **Formula**: \(\text{Realized Price} = \frac{\text{Gross Revenue} - \text{Total Discounts}}{\text{Units Sold}}\)
- **Business Meaning**: Actual net cash revenue captured per unit after deducting all off-invoice discounts, rebates, and markdowns.
- **Data Fields Required**: `fact_transactions.gross_revenue_inr`, `fact_transactions.total_discount_inr`, `fact_transactions.quantity`

### 6. Discount Waterfall Rate (%)
- **Formula**: \(\text{Discount \%} = \frac{\text{Total Discounts}}{\text{Gross Revenue}} \times 100\)
- **Business Meaning**: Percentage of top-line gross list value lost to contract discounts, promotional sales, and ad-hoc coupons.
- **Data Fields Required**: `fact_transactions.total_discount_inr`, `fact_transactions.gross_revenue_inr`

### 7. Gross Profit
- **Formula**: \(\text{Gross Profit} = \text{Net Revenue} - \text{Base COGS}\)
- **Business Meaning**: Monetary profit remaining after subtracting direct product manufacturing and procurement acquisition costs.
- **Data Fields Required**: `fact_transactions.net_revenue_inr`, `fact_transactions.base_cogs_inr`

### 8. Gross Margin (%)
- **Formula**: \(\text{Gross Margin \%} = \frac{\text{Net Revenue} - \text{Base COGS}}{\text{Net Revenue}} \times 100\)
- **Business Meaning**: Percentage of net revenue retained as gross profit to cover channel fulfillment, operating spend, and fixed overhead.
- **Data Fields Required**: `fact_transactions.net_revenue_inr`, `fact_transactions.base_cogs_inr`

### 9. Variable Cost
- **Formula**: \(\text{Variable Cost} = \text{Base COGS} + \text{Fulfillment Cost} + \text{Return Cost} + \text{Trade Promo Spend} - \text{Vendor Co-Op Rebate}\)
- **Business Meaning**: Total direct costs that scale directly with unit volume and transaction execution.
- **Data Fields Required**: `base_cogs_inr`, `fulfillment_cost_inr`, `return_cost_inr`, `promo_discount_inr`, `vendor_coop_rebate_inr`

### 10. Contribution Margin (%)
- **Formula**: \(\text{Contribution Margin \%} = \frac{\text{Net Revenue} - \text{Variable Cost}}{\text{Net Revenue}} \times 100\)
- **Business Meaning**: Percentage of net revenue available to pay fixed company operating overhead and generate net profit.
- **Data Fields Required**: `fact_transactions.net_revenue_inr`, Variable Cost components

### 11. Net Pocket Profit
- **Formula**: \(\text{Pocket Profit} = \text{Net Revenue} - \text{COGS} - \text{Fulfillment Cost} - \text{Return Cost} + \text{Vendor Co-Op Rebate}\)
- **Business Meaning**: True net bottom-line cash profit generated by a transaction after accounting for all direct revenue leakages and cost-to-serve expenses.
- **Data Fields Required**: `net_revenue_inr`, `base_cogs_inr`, `fulfillment_cost_inr`, `return_cost_inr`, `vendor_coop_rebate_inr`

### 12. Net Pocket Margin (%)
- **Formula**: \(\text{Pocket Margin \%} = \frac{\text{Net Pocket Profit}}{\text{Net Revenue}} \times 100\)
- **Business Meaning**: Ultimate measure of transaction profitability indicating net percentage margin delivered to operating profit.
- **Data Fields Required**: `pocket_profit_inr`, `net_revenue_inr`

### 13. Average Order Value (AOV)
- **Formula**: \(\text{AOV} = \frac{\text{Total Net Revenue}}{\text{Total Orders}}\)
- **Business Meaning**: Average net monetary size of customer shopping baskets per transaction checkout.
- **Data Fields Required**: `fact_transactions.net_revenue_inr`, `fact_transactions.transaction_id`

### 14. Orders Count
- **Formula**: \(\text{Orders} = \text{COUNT(DISTINCT transaction\_id)}\)
- **Business Meaning**: Total volume of distinct completed purchase transactions.
- **Data Fields Required**: `fact_transactions.transaction_id`

### 15. Active Customers
- **Formula**: \(\text{Customers} = \text{COUNT(DISTINCT customer\_id)}\)
- **Business Meaning**: Total count of unique individual or corporate buyer accounts placing at least one order during the period.
- **Data Fields Required**: `fact_transactions.customer_id`

### 16. Customer Repeat Purchase Rate (%)
- **Formula**: \(\text{Repeat Rate \%} = \frac{\text{Customers with } >1 \text{ Order}}{\text{Total Active Customers}} \times 100\)
- **Business Meaning**: Percentage of active customer base making multiple purchases, reflecting account loyalty and recurring demand.
- **Data Fields Required**: `fact_transactions.customer_id`, `fact_transactions.transaction_id`

### 17. Customer Net Margin
- **Formula**: \(\text{Customer Margin} = \sum_{\text{Cust}} \text{Pocket Profit}\)
- **Business Meaning**: Total cumulative net profit contribution delivered by a specific customer account across all historical orders.
- **Data Fields Required**: `customer_profitability_mart.total_pocket_profit_inr`

### 18. Competitor Price Index
- **Formula**: \(\text{Price Index} = \frac{\text{OmniRetail List Price}}{\text{Competitor Benchmark Price}}\)
- **Business Meaning**: Ratio comparing OmniRetail's list pricing against direct market competitors (e.g. Amazon India, Reliance Retail, D-Mart). An index < 1.0 indicates OmniRetail is cheaper.
- **Data Fields Required**: `dim_products.list_price_inr`, `fact_competitor_prices.competitor_price_inr`

### 19. Incremental Promotion ROI
- **Formula**: \(\text{Promo ROI} = \frac{\text{Incremental Gross Margin} - \text{Net Trade Spend}}{\text{Net Trade Spend}}\)
- **Business Meaning**: Financial return earned per Rupee of net promotional discount spend after subtracting estimated baseline sales margin.
- **Data Fields Required**: `promo_discount_inr`, `vendor_coop_rebate_inr`, Baseline sales estimates

### 20. Price Elasticity of Demand (\(\epsilon\))
- **Formula**: \(\epsilon = \frac{\% \Delta \text{ Quantity Demanded}}{\% \Delta \text{ Realized Price}} = \frac{\partial \ln Q}{\partial \ln P}\)
- **Business Meaning**: Sensitivity metric measuring percentage change in unit sales volume resulting from a 1% change in effective unit price.
- **Data Fields Required**: `fact_transactions.quantity`, `fact_transactions.net_revenue_inr`
