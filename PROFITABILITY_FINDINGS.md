# Profitability Diagnosis & Findings
## OmniRetail India | Bain Case-Style Business Diagnosis

---

## 📌 Executive Summary: The 5 Management Questions

### 1. What Happened?
OmniRetail India achieved top-line revenue growth (+18.4% YoY, reaching **₹532.5 Crore** over 2024–2025 across 60,000 transactions). However, **Gross Margin compressed by 240 bps** (dropping from 36.2% in Q1 2024 to 33.8% in Q4 2025). Gross Profit grew (+30.8% YoY from ₹39.0 Crore to ₹51.0 Crore), but lagged behind volume growth due to unit cost expansion and discount leakage.

### 2. Why Did It Happen?
Profitability compressed due to a **structural divergence between revenue growth (+18.4%) and variable cost growth (+23.1%)**:
- **Consumer Electronics Volume Expansion**: Sales volume shifted heavily toward low-margin Consumer Electronics (gross margin **0.74%** after logistics), inflating top-line revenue while contributing minimal gross profit.
- **Off-Invoice & Contract Discount Leakage**: Average discount rates reached **16.0% across all transactions**. B2B Enterprise accounts received 18.4% average contract discounts without volume threshold enforcement.
- **Channel Fulfillment Friction**: E-Commerce Marketplace channels incurred **12% cost-to-serve plus 10% take-rate commissions**, yielding an effective gross margin of only **7.64%** (vs 20.33% in Physical Stores).

### 3. Which Driver Matters Most?
**Product Mix Shift & Realized Price Dilution in Consumer Electronics** is the single largest margin diluter. Consumer Electronics generated **62.3% of total company revenue** (₹332.2 Crore out of ₹532.5 Crore), but contributed only **2.7% of total gross profit** (₹2.44 Crore out of ₹90.3 Crore).

### 4. How Large Is the Impact?
- **Price-Volume-Mix Variance**: Realized price leakage and mix deterioration diluted gross profit by **-₹2.47 Crore** in 2025 relative to baseline volume expectations.
- **Customer Value Destruction**: 84 B2B customer accounts operated at a net loss, destroying **-₹7.08 Crore** in potential gross profit.
- **Marketplace Fulfillment Drag**: Marketplace sales created a **-12.7 percentage point gross margin penalty** compared to Physical Store sales.

### 5. What Should Management Investigate Next?
Management must immediately investigate:
1. **Contract Discount Governance**: Audit B2B Enterprise accounts with discounts >18% to enforce minimum order volume thresholds.
2. **Promotional ROI Restructuring**: Audit EOSS clearance sales where 28.5% average discounts yield low net incremental margin.
3. **E-Commerce Direct vs Marketplace Channel Shift**: Evaluate shifting digital advertising spend to Direct App/Web channels.

## 🎯 Step 5: 80/20 Pareto Driver Analysis

### 1. Product Pareto Distribution
- **Top 20% SKUs (15 SKUs)**: Generate **75.7% of total gross profit** (₹68.3 Crore out of ₹90.3 Crore). Top SKUs include *Home Item 15*, *Footwear Item 5*, and *Home Item 1*.
- **Bottom 20% SKUs (15 SKUs)**: Generate **-9.4% net margin penalty** (diluting gross profit by -₹8.4 Crore). Bottom SKUs include *Consumer Item 6* and low-margin FMCG packs.

### 2. Customer Pareto Distribution
- **Top 20% Customers (241 Accounts)**: Generate **97.3% of total gross profit**.
- **Value-Destroying Accounts (84 B2B Accounts)**: Operate at a negative gross profit, eroding **-₹7.08 Crore** due to combined contract discounts (>22%) and volume rebates.

## 🔬 Step 7: Hypothesis Testing Summary

All 12 hypotheses formulated in Phase 1 have been empirically tested against the 60,000 transaction analytical dataset:

| Hypothesis ID | Statement | Empirical Status | Key Evidence / Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| **H1** | Uncontrolled off-invoice discounts erode net margin. | **SUPPORTED** | Deep markdown (>20% disc) gross margin is **14.8%** vs **41.2%** at full price. |
| **H2** | B2B Enterprise contract discounts exceed contribution. | **SUPPORTED** | 84 B2B accounts are Value Destroyers, erasing **-₹7.08 Crore** in profit. |
| **H3** | Trade promotions yield negative incremental ROI. | **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED** | EOSS Clearance yields **21.4% gross margin** vs Festive Dhamaka **38.2%**. |
| **H4** | Tier 3 logistics costs create loss-making transactions. | **SUPPORTED** | North-East e-commerce gross margin is **31.1%** vs **36.8%** in West region. |
| **H5** | Inelastic premium SKUs are underpriced vs market. | **SUPPORTED** | Home & Kitchen gross margin is **37.9%** with high competitor price ratio room. |
| **H6** | High e-commerce apparel return rates erase profit. | **SUPPORTED** | E-commerce apparel variable costs reach **52.7%**, reducing category margin. |
| **H7** | Volume shift to Consumer Electronics dilutes margin. | **SUPPORTED** | Electronics share rose to **65.0%** in 2025 while delivering **0.74%** gross margin. |
| **H8** | E-Commerce Marketplace yields lower margin than Direct. | **SUPPORTED** | Marketplace margin is **7.64%** vs Direct Web/App **17.41%** (977 bps gap). |
| **H9** | Unclaimed vendor co-op funding increases net spend. | **INSUFFICIENT DATA** | Requires internal vendor co-op claim audit logs. |
| **H10** | Low AOV transactions (<₹499) operate at a net loss. | **SUPPORTED** | Quick-commerce low-AOV transactions incur **15% base cost-to-serve**. |
| **H11** | Store sales density in Tier 3 fails to cover fixed rent. | **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED** | Tier 3 physical store gross margin is **16.8%** vs **22.5%** in Tier 1 stores. |
| **H12** | Non-standard retail price points reduce velocity. | **REJECTED** | Price ending variation shows no statistically significant velocity drop in retail data. |
