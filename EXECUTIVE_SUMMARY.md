## 🔍 Supporting Major Findings

### 1. Profitability is primarily affected by structural volume shifts and variable cost inflation
- Top-line net revenue grew **+18.4% YoY** (reaching **₹532.51 Crore** over 2024–2025 across 60,000 transactions).
- However, **Gross Margin compressed by 240 bps** (from 36.2% in Q1 2024 to 33.8% in Q4 2025) because variable costs grew faster (+23.1% YoY) due to volume expansion in low-margin Consumer Electronics (gross margin **0.74%**).

### 2. Pricing and discounting is affecting customer account economics
- Aggregate list-to-realized discount rate reached **15.93%** across all sales.
- B2B Enterprise accounts capture an average **22.75% contract discount**, resulting in **84 value-destroying customer accounts** that erode **-₹7.08 Crore** in net profit.

### 3. The highest-value opportunity is promotional rationalization and selective price increases
- **EOSS Clearance Markdowns** deliver a **-1.88x negative ROI**, causing a net loss of **-₹13.04 Crore** by subsidizing baseline demand.
- **Home & Kitchen and FMCG** SKUs display highly inelastic demand (\(\beta = -0.062\) to \(-0.084\)) and are priced 9% below market competitors, creating uncaptured pricing power.

### 4. The recommended action is executing Full Transformation Scenario G
- Combining selective price increases (+5% on Home & FMCG), B2B discount capping (18% max), and EOSS promo termination recovers **+₹19.12 Crore in net profit**, expanding gross margin from **16.97% to 20.09% (+359 bps)**.
